# Conto Product Vision

## Vision

Conto is a platform that transforms the way people discover, choose, and experience bars, cafés, breweries, and restaurants. Instead of jumping between Google Maps, Instagram, menus, and messages, people can discover venues, book tables, pre-order, and enjoy a seamless experience from the moment they decide to go out until they leave. Our vision is to become the default platform people open before deciding where to go.

## The Problem

Today, discovering a good place to eat or drink is fragmented. Users typically search on Google Maps, check Instagram to see if the place "looks nice," read inconsistent reviews, or look for photos or PDFs of menus. This also affects businesses, which struggle to attract recurring customers, understand their audience, and create personalized experiences.

## The Solution

Conto unifies everything into a single experience.

**Customers** can discover places based on their preferences, book a table, pre-order food and drinks, order from their phone, and earn rewards (ContoCoins). They also receive personalized recommendations.

**Businesses**, on the other hand, have tools to manage menus, handle reservations, launch promotions, understand customer behavior, and build loyalty.

## Target Audience

Our target audience includes people who enjoy going out and want a faster, simpler, and more personalized experience. This includes friends, couples, families, tourists, food lovers, and coffee enthusiasts.

On the business side, we focus on small to medium-sized bars, restaurants, breweries, and food trucks.

## Guiding Principles

Every decision at Conto must be:

- Simple rather than complex
- Fast rather than feature-heavy
- Personalized without being invasive
- Mobile-first
- Friendly
- With the user always in control
- Above all: explore first, register later

## MVP Goal

The goal of the MVP is to validate that people want to discover places with Conto, book tables, and experience something better than traditional alternatives.

## Long-Term Vision

In the long term, Conto aims to become the go-to operating system for going out — helping people discover places they truly enjoy, and providing businesses with modern tools to create memorable experiences and lasting relationships with their customers.
